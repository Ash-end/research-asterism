# Evidence and capabilities

For field learning, skim an initial batch at a common depth, classify by operative mechanisms and read one family through time before expanding targeted searches. No fixed paper quota establishes coverage. Use clear explanations to reach the decisive equations, proof dependencies or protocol. A source report, conditional derivation and explicit conjecture can all inform method properties when labeled; only actual outcome evidence supplies an empirical performance estimate. Retrieve when a consequential premise needs checking, not to postpone every useful construction. See [learning path](learning-path.md).


## Inspect capabilities before depending on them

Use the host's available tools, documentation, and current permissions to determine whether retrieval, full-text reading, image/table inspection, computation, and controlled execution are possible. Do not infer availability from a tool name in a paper, an installed credential, or another project. Use an appropriate available tool; no particular MCP is required.

| Capability | Useful work | If unavailable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retrieval | Locate primary papers, origins, rivals, corrections, or negative evidence | Analyze provided materials and label the map and novelty assessment provisional |
| Reading | Inspect the actual claim, assumptions, protocol, decisive table/figure or proof | State abstract/excerpt-only coverage and unresolved details |
| Analysis | Check calculations, uncertainty, counterexamples, or controlled synthetic examples | Derive a transparent plan and distinguish hand calculation from execution |
| Experiment execution | Run the specific currently authorized validation | Deliver the design; do not claim it ran or resume an old run |

Before an external call, determine what content it sends and whether current authorization covers the data and any fees. Prefer minimal public bibliographic queries where possible. If a permission or supported access route fails, report the precise blocker and continue work that does not depend on it. Do not bypass access controls, guess storage URLs, or silently substitute a paid provider. A read or experiment plan is not blanket authorization to publish or upload.

## Bind each claim to its actual support

For a consequential evidence item keep a compact record, in the response or existing project document:

`claim | source identifier + locator | inspected scope | reported conditions | supported conclusion | limitation/status`

Use a DOI or official URL when verified, or an exact supplied artifact and locator. Never invent identifiers. A synthetic source label must remain visibly synthetic. Verify both identity and support: a valid DOI does not show that a paper establishes the cited conclusion. Check whether evidence is a reported correlation, controlled comparison, theorem under conditions, or an author's interpretation. Inspect decisive figures and tables, not only surrounding prose, when available.

Use three labels in consequential judgments:

- **Source report:** what the inspected material reports or the artifact shows, with its population, conditions and inspected scope; not automatic verification of the report.
- **Inference:** your conclusion from stated evidence and assumptions, including alternatives.
- **Untested hypothesis:** a proposed explanation or prediction requiring new evidence.

Evidence may support one clause and leave another unverified. Say which. Do not replace this distinction with a single confidence number. Record unavailable full text, ambiguous metadata, failed retrieval, or conflicting reports as limitations. Corrections and retractions can change evidence status, so check current source status when that matters and retrieval is available.

## State coverage honestly

Keep search date, sources, important query families, inclusion scope, and unresolved coverage proportional to the decision. Search until the decisive ambiguity is resolved or the authorized budget is reached; no fixed count proves completeness. No result in a bounded search means only no direct evidence was located within that search. Describe the nearest known comparison without claiming that nothing else exists.

Separate repository popularity and polished output from demonstrated scientific or skill effectiveness. Structural validation checks packaging. Behavioral evaluations sample reasoning. Neither proves research novelty, correctness across fields, or publication prospects.
